# Code of Conduct

Harsha's Amazing Call Center is a technical community for building safer, more reliable realtime voice systems. Participation should make it easier for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience to contribute.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, constructive, and respectful.
- Critique claims, code, protocols, and evidence—not people.
- State uncertainty and distinguish observed behavior from inference.
- Protect caller privacy. Never post credentials, private recordings, transcripts, phone numbers, customer data, or unredacted provider payloads.
- Get explicit consent before contacting people, opening paid provider sessions, or testing against infrastructure you do not own.
- Report security vulnerabilities privately as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate privacy violations, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community or its users. Reports may be sent through the private channels in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will limit disclosure to what is necessary to investigate and respond.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, community spaces, and public events where someone is representing the project.
